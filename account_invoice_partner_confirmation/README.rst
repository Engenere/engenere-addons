This module is for firms managing partner goods and invoice receipts.

With it, it is possible to link the delivery of the goods and the
invoice to: - Date the partner confirmed receipt - Employees responsible
for the transaction - Vehicle that transported the goods referring to
the invoice - Issues to be resolved - Who received the note and related
goods - Delivery receipts or other related documents
