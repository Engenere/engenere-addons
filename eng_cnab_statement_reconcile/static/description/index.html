Engenere CNAB Statement Reconcile

This module reconciles the bank statement with the CNAB clearing lines of eng_cnab_clearing, so that each collection credit of the bank statement settles exactly the liquidations the bank credited on that day.

A reconciliation model with CNAB Clearing Matching matches a statement line with the whole group of open CNAB clearing lines that:

  • are in the clearing account of the journal of the statement line, of the same company and currency, and posted;
  • are due (date_maturity) on the date of the statement line, which is the credit date of the liquidations;
  • belong to the receiving bank class of the rule: any bank, the bank of the journal only, or the other banks only, for the banks that split the collection credit of a day by receiving bank.

The statement line is reconciled only when the whole group sums exactly its amount. There is no search for a subset of the group, no tolerance and no write-off. Only a statement line that is still fully open is matched.

The routing is exclusive: a statement line that meets the conditions of a CNAB rule is matched by the CNAB rules only. When its group does not close, no other reconciliation model is tried, so that a manual receipt of the same amount in the same account is never consumed by chance. The routing holds even when the CNAB configuration of the journal has become invalid since it was saved, for instance when the account of its CNAB receipt method was cleared: the line then gets no proposal at all. When the CNAB rules that route the same line close distinct groups of its exact amount, none of them is applied. Neither is a group of its exact amount that another fully open collection credit of the same day also meets under one of these rules: no rule wins the line by its sequence.

The matching runs:

  • when the statement line is created, through the automatic reconciliation of account_reconcile_oca;
  • after each CNAB return import, by a trigger of a scheduled action that runs after the import, in a transaction of its own: the import never reconciles anything by itself;
  • every 30 minutes, by the same scheduled action, for every pending line;
  • from the Retry CNAB Matching button of the statement line.

Each collection credit shows the reason it is still open: awaiting the CNAB return, amount difference (with the difference), partially reconciled, ambiguous (another fully open collection credit of the same day meets the same rule, or two rules close distinct groups), invalid configuration of the journal, unreconciled by hand, or error; or that it is reconciled. A reconciliation made by hand on the reconciliation screen clears the reason.

A statement line unreconciled by hand leaves the CNAB matching: neither the scheduled action nor the automatic reconciliation match it again, until its Retry CNAB Matching button.

Concurrent attempts lock the statement line and then its candidate clearing lines in id order. A concurrency error is never caught: the whole transaction is repeated, by the RPC layer for the button or by the next run of the scheduled action. The scheduled action runs each line in a savepoint of its own, so that a business error of one line is shown as its reason while the other lines go on.

The module also refuses a bank statement file in which a transaction already imported comes back with the same identifier (the FITID of an OFX file) but another date or amount. The same transaction imported again with the same content is still skipped, as before.

Configuration

  1. Configure the CNAB clearing flow of the bank journal (module eng_cnab_clearing). The journal must reconcile its statement lines in the Edit Move mode, and its suspense account cannot be the clearing account.
  2. Create one reconciliation model (Invoicing > Configuration > Reconciliation Models) for each collection credit of the bank statement:
    • type Rule to match invoices/bills, with Auto-validate;
    • the bank journal, the amount type Received and a label condition that only the collection credit meets;
    • CNAB Clearing Matching enabled;
    • the Receiving Bank filter: Any Bank when the bank credits the collection of a day at once, or Own Bank and Other Banks in two rules when the statement splits it by receiving bank.
  3. Give the CNAB rules a sequence lower than the other rules.
  4. The tariffs and the movements of an investment account can be posted by rules of the type Rule to suggest counterpart entry, with Auto-validate.

The scheduled action CNAB Clearing: Reconcile Pending Bank Statement Lines runs every 30 minutes; its interval can be changed.

Usage

Import the CNAB return files and the bank statement files as usual, in any order:

  • when the CNAB return comes first, the collection credit of the statement is reconciled as soon as its line is created;
  • when the bank statement comes first, its collection credit waits for the return, and the scheduled action reconciles it after the import of the return.

On the reconciliation screen, a pending collection credit shows its reason and the Retry CNAB Matching button; the list of statement lines shows the status, and the filter CNAB Pending lists every pending one. A difference or an ambiguity is never adjusted automatically: review the line on the reconciliation screen, where it can still be reconciled by hand. A line you unreconcile stays with you until you press Retry CNAB Matching.

A bank statement file is refused when one of its transactions was already imported with the same identifier but another date or amount: check the file with the bank.

Known issues / Roadmap

  • The Keep Suspense Accounts reconcile mode needs its own tests before it is supported.
  • Two simultaneous attempts on the same group, from two sessions, cannot be tested in the CI, where no two transactions see the test data: the lock and the retry of the transaction are covered there, the concurrent case must be checked on a test database.
